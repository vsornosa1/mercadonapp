Statement of Intent
Outcome: An app on my phone that tells me the truth about what I'm buying at Mercadona — photo, calories, macros, and how processed/additive-heavy it is — and points me to a similar but better alternative, so my cart drifts toward whole products and fewer ultraprocesados.

User: Just me at first. Not hard-coded to one user (others will use it later), but no accounts, login, or multi-user anything in v1.

Why now: Nothing dramatic — I want to eat healthier (whole, less processed, more variety) and hold/gain muscle, and nothing helps me at the moment I'm actually choosing a product in the aisle.

Success: A month in, my cart has noticeably fewer ultra-processed products and I stop second-guessing myself at the shelf.

Constraint: Everything rests on product data — catalogue, photos, and above all nutrition and ingredients, from the Mercadona API. If that API exposes only name/price/photo, then calories, macros and additives must be enriched from a second source (most likely Open Food Facts, matched by barcode). Second constraint: used one-handed, on a phone, standing in a shop — so no multi-step flows.

Out of scope (v1): Recipes and menu building (deferred deliberately — next layer, not dropped); basket/quantity totals; price comparison and price history; online ordering/checkout; barcode scanning and receipt photos; accounts and other users; native/store app.
